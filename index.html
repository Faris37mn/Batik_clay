Batik Clay Perak Heritage

Authentic Clay-Resist Artistry.

Wallago Collection preserves the unique craft of Batik Tanah Liat (Clay-Resist Batik) originating from Perak. Using natural riverbed clay as a resist technique instead of traditional hot wax, each yard of fabric yields distinct organic textures, earthy tones, and one-of-a-kind wearable art. Connect with our atelier via WhatsApp for custom fabric lengths and personalized tailoring.
